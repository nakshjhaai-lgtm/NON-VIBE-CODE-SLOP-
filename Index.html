Everything You Need to Know

NetGuard operates at the DNS level—the internet's address book. When any device on your network tries to visit a website, it first asks our DNS servers for that site's address.

If the site is gambling-related, we simply don't provide the address. The connection never happens. The page never loads. This happens in under 50 milliseconds, before any content reaches the device. It's invisible, instant, and impossible to bypass without router access.

Unlike browser extensions or apps that can be disabled with two clicks, NetGuard works at the network infrastructure level. The only ways around it are:

  • Having administrator access to the router (which you control)
  • Using mobile data instead of WiFi
  • Using a VPN (detected and blocked on Business/Enterprise plans)

Our Business plan includes VPN and proxy detection that closes that loophole. We're constantly evolving to stay ahead of bypass attempts.

Most users actually experience faster internet! Here's why:

  • Our DNS servers are globally distributed and optimized for speed (<50ms response)
  • We block resource-heavy ads and trackers that slow down page loads
  • Malicious scripts never execute, reducing browser overhead

In our user surveys, 73% report faster browsing after switching to NetGuard.

No installation required on any device. That's the beauty of DNS-level protection. You configure it once on your router, and every device that connects to your WiFi is automatically protected—phones, tablets, laptops, gaming consoles, smart TVs, everything. Zero maintenance, zero updates to manage.

Administrators can whitelist any domain instantly from the dashboard. You can also:

  • Create time-based exceptions (e.g., allow during business hours only)
  • Set user-specific rules (different access for different profiles)
  • Report false positives to improve our AI (usually fixed within hours)

Our false positive rate is under 0.01%—among the lowest in the industry.
